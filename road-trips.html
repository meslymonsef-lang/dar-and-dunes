CITY TO SEA

The Atlantic pause

Trade city energy for sea air. Add nights where you feel like staying, rather than trying to see everything in one day.

Marrakech → Essaouira → Agadir

Start in Marrakech ↗
  1. 01

    Marrakech

    Settle in before collecting your car. Ask about pickup access and parking outside pedestrian lanes.

  2. 02

    Essaouira

    Make space for harbor walks and a slower day. Confirm overnight parking with your accommodation.

  3. 03

    Agadir

    Stay by the coast. If returning the car here, check one-way availability and fees before booking.

A JOURNEY, NOT A DAY TRIP

Toward the dunes

Build in overnight stops and daylight driving. Ask local hosts about current road and weather conditions before each leg.

Marrakech → Ouarzazate → Merzouga

Plan the desert journey ↗
  1. 01

    Marrakech

    Review the rental agreement and plan a manageable first driving day rather than rushing toward the desert.

  2. 02

    Ouarzazate

    Use an overnight stop to break the journey. Recheck onward driving times with current maps.

  3. 03

    Merzouga

    Arrange the final transfer with your host. Do not take a standard rental car onto sand or unapproved roads.

CITIES, THEN MOUNTAIN AIR

The northern notebook

Pair time in the medina with a quieter mountain-town stay. Keep driving days separate from your most ambitious sightseeing plans.

Casablanca → Fes → Chefchaouen

Start in Casablanca ↗
  1. 01

    Casablanca

    Confirm the exact collection point and allow time after your arrival before driving.

  2. 02

    Fes

    Stay long enough for a medina visit. Ask your host where to leave the car and how to reach your accommodation.

  3. 03

    Chefchaouen

    Take mountain roads at a comfortable pace. Check road conditions and leave plenty of daylight.

Routes are inspiration, not verified navigation. Check current maps, weather, road access and rental restrictions. No driving times or live rental availability are provided.

BEFORE THE KEYS CHANGE HANDS

A little preparation.
A lot more freedom.

01

Know the full cost

Confirm insurance excess, deposit, mileage limits, fuel policy and one-way charges.

02

Know what to bring

Ask the provider which license, identification, payment card and driver-age requirements apply.

03

Check before you leave

Record existing damage and check the vehicle, emergency contact and return arrangements.

04

Keep a little margin

Leave room for stops and changing conditions. Save offline maps and avoid a rushed arrival.
