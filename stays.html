01 / THE RIAD

Behind the medina walls

For travelers who love an intimate setting and a sense of place. Look closely at room layouts, stairs, courtyard noise and how you will reach the entrance with luggage.

  • Ask about airport transfers and arrival access
  • Confirm air conditioning and room facilities
  • Check whether breakfast is included

A little more room to be you

BEFORE YOU UNPACK

A better stay starts
with better questions.

Keep these details close when you compare real offers.

Can I book accommodation here?

Not yet. Dar & Dunes is a travel-planning preview. You can save a destination and trip dates, but availability, prices and reservations are not connected.

What should the total price include?

Ask the provider to list the room or rental cost, taxes, cleaning charges, service fees and optional extras. Clarify which amounts are refundable and when payment is due.

How do I choose a suitable location?

Think about arrival access, walking distances and what you want to do. Ask the host about stairs, parking and step-free access instead of relying on the map alone.

Where are my saved plans kept?

Your collection is stored in this browser, not in an online account. It does not sync across devices. Clearing site data will remove it.
